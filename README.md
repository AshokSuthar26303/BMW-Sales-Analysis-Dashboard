# 🚗 BMW Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project is an interactive **BMW Sales Analysis Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Power BI**.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of BMW sales performance across different years, models, months, and regions. It combines key performance indicators with interactive visualizations to identify sales trends and business insights.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze BMW sales performance over time
- Compare revenue generated by different BMW models
- Identify top-selling BMW models
- Analyze monthly unit sales trends
- Understand the relationship between car price and units sold
- Track yearly EV growth
- Compare BMW unit sales across different regions
- Build an interactive and user-friendly business intelligence dashboard

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI**
- **Power Query**
- **DAX**
- **Data Visualization**

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 💰 Revenue by Model

A donut chart showing the revenue contribution of each BMW model.

### 🏆 Top Selling Models

A bar chart comparing the unit sales of different BMW models.

### 📅 Monthly Unit Sold

A line chart showing monthly BMW unit sales and sales trends.

### 💵 Car Price vs Unit Sold

A scatter chart analyzing the relationship between average car price and units sold for different BMW models.

### ⚡ Yearly EV Growth

A line chart showing the growth of EV share across the years.

### 🌎 Unit Sold by Region

A regional comparison of BMW unit sales across:

- China
- Rest of World
- USA
- Europe
